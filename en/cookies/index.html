Cookie policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer or phone. They help the website work or provide insight into how it is used.

Which cookies do we use?

  • Necessary: the cookie im_consent remembers your cookie choice, so you do not see the notice on every visit. Retention: 12 months. This cookie is always on.
  • Statistics: we currently do not use statistics cookies.
  • Marketing: we currently do not use marketing cookies.

If we use statistics or marketing cookies in the future, they will only be placed after you have given your consent.

Third-party services

The fonts on this website are loaded via Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address; Google does not place cookies for this. Videos and photos are served from our own website. The links to Facebook and Instagram take you to those websites, where their own cookie rules apply.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the button below or via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
