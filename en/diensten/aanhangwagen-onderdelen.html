A trailer is only as strong as its parts

Trailers are indispensable means of transport. With the right components, your trailer drives, brakes and loads safely and efficiently, day in, day out. Always use the right parts and check and maintain them regularly for safe and efficient use of your trailer. The main categories we produce and supply:

Coupling and tow hitch

The connection between vehicle and trailer must be one hundred percent reliable. We supply couplings and tow hitches matched to the weight and use of your trailer.

Overrun brake

The overrun brake ensures the trailer brakes independently along with the towing vehicle, essential for safety and legally required above certain weights.

Axles and wheels

Axles and wheels determine the load capacity and driving behavior. We supply versions matched to the total weight and intended use.

Lighting

Good lighting is mandatory and crucial for visibility. From tail light units to marker lighting.

Load bed and closed side panels

We make load beds and side panels to measure, precisely matched to the dimensions and use of your trailer.

Ramp plates and loading ramps

For safely loading and unloading vehicles and machinery. Available in various lengths and load capacities.

Support legs

Support legs provide stability when loading, unloading and parking, robustly built and custom produced.
