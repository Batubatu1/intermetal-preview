The foundation of every chalet

Chalets are popular holiday homes: cosy, homely and set in the midst of nature. The chassis, also known as the foundation, is the base on which the entire structure rests. It ensures the chalet does not sink or tilt on uneven ground.

Resistant to wind and weather

A chalet chassis must not only be sturdy and stable, but also resistant to changing weather conditions. Harsh conditions can cause a chassis to wear faster than at a home in a sheltered location, which is exactly why the quality of the steel and finish matters so much.

Custom-manufactured

All in all, the chassis is a defining component in the construction and performance of a chalet. Intermetal manufactures your chalet chassis entirely to your wishes and specifications, in-house.
