01 · Parts

Trailer parts

From ramp plates, couplings, tow hitches and load beds to side panels: we can produce and/or supply any part you need.

A trailer is only as strong as its parts. With the right components, your trailer drives, brakes and loads safely and efficiently. Always use the right parts and check and maintain them regularly for safe and efficient use of your trailer.

  • Coupling and tow hitch
  • Overrun brake
  • Axles and wheels
  • Lighting
  • Load bed and closed side panels
  • Ramp plates and loading ramps
  • Support legs

Tiny house chassis

Chassis from 6 to 10 metres, with a load capacity from 750 kg to 3850 kg and more.

The chassis is the structure your tiny house rests on: steel tubes or beams that carry the full weight. Flexibility, material quality and proper assembly determine how well your house holds up over the years. Intermetal produces every chassis entirely to your specifications.

03 · Chalets

Chalet chassis

Chassis from 6 to 10 metres, with a load capacity from 750 kg to 3850 kg and more, entirely to your requirements.

The chassis is the foundation the whole chalet rests on: it prevents subsidence or tilting on uneven ground. A chalet chassis must not only be sturdy and stable, but also weatherproof. That is why Intermetal builds them to measure, from quality steel.
